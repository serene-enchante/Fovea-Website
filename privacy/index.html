Legal & Policies

Operational guidelines, terms of use, privacy practices, and scientific disclaimers for Fovea.

Overview

Fovea is a decentralized citizen science spatial data platform designed to make count circle observations and geographic boundaries accessible, verifiable, and transparent. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimal data necessary to maintain spatial accuracy and support interactive mapping.

Suggestion Form & Map Annotations

When you use the Suggest Edit tool to submit circle notes, report boundary discrepancies, or draw spatial annotations (markers, lines, or polygons) on the map:

Google Sheets Intake & Routing

Information entered into the suggestion form routes directly to a Google Sheets form backend via Google Apps Script.

  • Information Collected: Submissions include the subject/title, description text, spatial coordinates of drawn annotations (GeoJSON features), timestamp, and any contact information (such as an email address) that you voluntarily supply.
  • Site Manager's Discretion: All feedback and suggested boundaries submitted through the form are reviewed by the site manager. At the site manager's discretion, submitted suggestions and annotations may be incorporated into public spatial datasets, shared with local count circle compilers, or distributed to scientific researchers and conservation partners to correct spatial errors and improve the accuracy of citizen science data.
  • Contact Details: If you choose to provide an email address or contact info, it is used strictly for clarifying questions regarding the specifics of your suggested edit. Your contact information will never be sold, rented, or used for commercial marketing.

Local Browser Storage

Fovea uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to preserve visual preferences, such as your selected theme mode (Dark or Light) and local viewing states. This data remains on your device and is not tracked across websites.

Third-Party Infrastructure

Fovea utilizes the following external infrastructure to provide its services:

  • Google Workspace / Google Sheets: Used as the backend form processor for suggestion submissions.
  • MapLibre GL & Spatial Tile Servers: Used to render interactive 3D globe and 2D vector map layers.

Informational Use Only (Not a Legal Survey)

Notice: Not for Critical Navigation or Legal Boundaries

Map boundaries, count circle radii (e.g., 15-mile CBC diameters), center points, and coordinates displayed on Fovea are geographic approximations compiled for citizen science observation. They are NOT legal survey monuments, property tax boundaries, or nautical/aviation navigation tools.

Private Property & Trespass Prohibition

Count circles frequently overlay a mixture of public lands, private properties, conservation easements, and restricted reserves.

  • No Right of Entry: The display of any area within a count circle on Fovea does NOT grant permission, implied right-of-way, or authorization to enter upon private property.
  • Respect Signage: Users are strictly responsible for observing all posted signs, boundary markers, gates, and obtaining explicit landowner permission before accessing any non-public lands.
  • Trespass Liability: Neither Fovea nor count circle organizers assume any liability for individuals who trespass or enter restricted lands while participating in bird counts or field observation.
